Co-founder & CEO

Zalor

Sep 2025 – Present

I founded Zalor after noticing a gap in how teams build and ship AI agents. AI agents are increasingly responsible for real actions like creating records, triggering workflows, and interacting with production systems. But there was no reliable way to test changes before deployment.

After speaking with 45+ teams building AI agents, I found that most relied on manual testing of prompts, tools, and workflows, often only discovering failures after shipping to production.

To solve this, I built Zalor, a platform that uses AI agents to test other AI agents. It automatically generates high-coverage test scenarios, simulates real user behavior, and detects regressions before deployment.
